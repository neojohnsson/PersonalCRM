Before you connect Gmail

Google's permission screen comes next, and it is written by lawyers. This one is written by the person who built the app. Read this one first.

What it reads

Message headers — who wrote, when, and the subject line

Message bodies — only for the one thread you ask to summarise or reply to

What it keeps

Contact details, your notes, and message metadata — sender, date, subject, direction

Summaries you write or generate — every one editable and deletable

Not message bodies. Those stay in Gmail unless you opt in, one contact at a time.

What it will never do

Send an email. Drafts land in Gmail and you press send.

Track whether anyone opened or read a message

Worth knowing: Google's draft permission also technically permits sending. There is no send endpoint anywhere in this app — but you are granting the ability, so you should hear it from me rather than notice it on the next screen.
